HairSway

A DaVinci Resolve / Fusion Fuse for automatically swaying separated hair, ribbons, clothing hems, and similar layers without keyframes. It deforms the image locally along Viewer pins and supports up to eight independent Sways per node.

Automatic motion for hair and fabric

Place pins from root to tip, set the Influence Radius, and tune the motion. Separate Sways can be assigned to different strands while remaining inside one node.

DaVinci Resolve 21Free / StudioFuse

Key features

No keyframes required

Create continuous motion from distance, speed, phase, tip lag, and related controls.

Up to 8 Sways

Configure Sway 1–8 independently inside one HairSway node.

Root / Protection controls

Keep the scalp, face, and other fixed areas from deforming unintentionally.

Resolve 21 Free supported

Designed for DaVinci Resolve 21 Free and Studio.

Usage and parameters

The manual covers image preparation, pin placement, Sway 1–8, Protection Pins, and a practical adjustment order for natural motion.
