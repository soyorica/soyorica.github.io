General information about Soyorica. For installation and usage questions, see the FAQ for your product.

About Soyorica

What do you make?

  • Soyorica makes plugins for DaVinci Resolve / Fusion.
  • Products include MathMotionGraph / Lite for graphing, MathText for equations, and HairSway for adding sway to hair and fabric.
  • Find each product and its guides on the home page.

Shop & downloads

Where can I get the products?

  • Visit the Soyorica BOOTH shop.

Where can I find the terms of use?

  • See Using Our Products.
  • Contact us before use if anything is unclear.

Can a team share a product among multiple people?

  • As explained in Using Our Products, products may not be shared among multiple people.
  • If you would like to share a product within a team, contact soyorica.tools@gmail.com.

Installation and uninstallation

  • To install, close DaVinci Resolve, extract the ZIP, and run the supplied install.bat. Follow the same steps when updating.
  • To uninstall, close DaVinci Resolve and run the supplied uninstall.bat. If you run uninstall.bat by mistake, run install.bat again to reinstall.
  • If install.bat does not work, see your product’s Installation page.
    If uninstall.bat does not work, see “How do I update or uninstall the plugin?” in your product’s FAQ.
