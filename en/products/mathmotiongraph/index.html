Start with the free Lite edition

Lite includes Cartesian graphs, Moving Point, guides, value labels, and keyframable constants a–d. It has no watermark, time limit, or product-imposed resolution limit.

Full adds AND / OR, a second function, Parametric, Polar, Draw Start / End, Region Animation, and more.
