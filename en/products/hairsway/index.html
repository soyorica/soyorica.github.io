HairSway

A DaVinci Resolve / Fusion Fuse that automatically animates separated hair, ribbons, clothing hems, and similar layers without keyframes. Viewer pins define local deformation, with up to eight independent Sways per node.

01 / VIEWER

Pins from root to tip

Pin Count is 2–6. Start with the fixed Root Pin and place the remaining pins toward the tip.

02 / INSPECTOR

Tune the motion

Tune distance, speed, phase, Tip Lag, Root Lock, and related controls while playing the timeline.

Connect to your node graph
