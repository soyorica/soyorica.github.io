HairSway

Basic HairSway workflow and detailed Inspector controls, paired with actual DaVinci Resolve screens.
VERSION 1.2.3

Overview

HairSway is a DaVinci Resolve / Fusion Fuse that automatically animates separated illustration layers such as hair, ribbons, and clothing hems without keyframes. It deforms the image locally along a chain of Viewer pins and supports up to eight independent Sways in one node.

Prepare the image

For best results, separate the parts you want to animate onto transparent layers. Typical examples include front hair, side hair, back hair, ribbons, and clothing hems. If the source is not layer-separated, isolate the target area with a Fusion tool such as Polygon before HairSway.

Basic workflow

  1. Import the target layer into Fusion, add HairSway, and connect the image to its Input.
  2. Open Sway 1 and enable Enable Sway 1 and Show Pins in Viewer.
  3. Choose Pin Count. Place the Root Pin at the base, then arrange the remaining pins in order toward the tip.
  4. Reduce Influence Radius so it covers the target strand without unnecessary neighboring areas.
  5. Play the timeline and tune Sway Distance, Speed, Tip Lag, Roundness, Root Lock, and Root Protection.
When setting multiple strands, hide the pins for a completed Sway before showing the next one. This makes Viewer controls easier to manage.

Protection Pins

Protection Pins keep specific areas from being deformed by any Sway. They are useful for the top of the head, face, ears, hair base, ribbon knots, or fixed clothing. Up to four Protection Pins are available.

If the scalp or face moves, review the Root Pin position, then Root Lock, Root Protection, Influence Radius, and finally add a Protection Pin if needed.

Troubleshooting

The scalp or face moves

  1. Place the Root Pin exactly at the hair/scalp boundary.
  2. Increase Root Lock to roughly 0.30–0.45 and test.
  3. Increase Root Protection.
  4. Reduce Influence Radius to match the strand width.
  5. Add a Protection Pin when needed.

Playback is slow

Lower the Viewer proxy resolution, disable unused Sways, avoid unnecessary Sways, and temporarily disable other heavy nodes while editing. Return to full resolution for the final review.
