Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions before purchase and while using Soyorica tools.

Does HairSway work with the free edition of DaVinci Resolve?
Yes. The HairSway documentation lists DaVinci Resolve 21 Free and Studio as supported editions.
Can HairSway work with a flattened illustration?
It can, but separated transparent layers are recommended. If the image is not separated, isolate the part you want to animate with a Fusion tool such as Polygon before HairSway.
HairSway is moving the face or scalp. What should I change?
Review the Root Pin position, then Root Lock, Root Protection, and Influence Radius. Add a Protection Pin when necessary.
Does MathMotionGraph Lite have a watermark?
No. The Lite edition has no watermark, time limit, or product-imposed resolution limit.
What is the difference between MathMotionGraph Lite and Full?
Lite supports one Cartesian function, one implicit equation, one inequality, Moving Point, and related display controls. Full adds AND/OR conditions, a second function, Parametric, Polar, Draw Start/End, Region Animation, and more.
Can MathMotionGraph be used on both Edit and Fusion pages?
Yes. The .setting file provides the Edit-page generator and the .fuse is the rendering tool used by Fusion. The Edit .setting still requires its matching .fuse.
I installed the files but the tool is missing.
Quit Resolve before copying, extract the ZIP, verify the destination folders and extensions, then restart Resolve. For MathMotionGraph, verify both the Fuse and Edit Generator locations.
How do I contact support?
Contact Soyorica through the BOOTH shop. Include the product name, OS, Resolve version, Free/Studio edition, whether you are using Fusion or Edit, reproduction steps, and the error message if available.

Still need help?

Contact Soyorica through the BOOTH shop. Product name, OS, Resolve version, reproduction steps, and error details help with investigation.
